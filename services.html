Our Healthcare Support Services

Comprehensive clinical and administrative services for medical facilities

1. Clinical & Procedural Support

  • Pre-operative patient preparation – vitals, skin prep, room set-up
  • In-procedure assistance – instrument handling, graft counting, microscope work
  • Post-operative wound care & dressing – immediate recovery support, patient discharge briefing
  • Sterilisation & equipment turnover – decontamination, autoclave cycles, logbook completion
  • Daily theatre safety checks – emergency equipment, sharps, COSHH compliance

2. Patient Coordination & Liaison

  • Initial consultation scheduling & triage calls – phone / video / email
  • GDPR-compliant consent management – info sheets, e-sign capture, secure archiving
  • Day-of-surgery patient reception & ID verification
  • Post-op follow-up (24h / 7d / 30d) – phone check-ins, wound-healing photo review
  • Interpreter or chaperone arrangement (if requested)

3. Media, Marketing & Content

  • Clinical photography & videography – before/after sets, procedural clips
  • Social-media asset creation – caption writing, short-form video edits, hashtag research
  • Weekly content calendar planning – clinic promotions, patient-journey stories
  • Performance analytics & KPI report – reach, engagement, conversion metrics
  • Reputation-management responses – Trustpilot/Google review replies, complaint triage

4. Compliance & Governance

  • CQC audit preparation & evidence collation – policy updates, training logs
  • Data-protection impact assessments (DPIA) – risk analysis, mitigation actions
  • Incident reporting & duty-of-candour documentation
  • Disposable-tracking & stock-expiry checks – batch/lot traceability
  • Policy drafting / revision – infection control, consent, social-media use

5. Administrative & Reporting (remote-friendly)

  • Daily clinic list & rota production – staff allocation aligned to case volume
  • Monthly activity & revenue report – case counts, spend per patient, OT utilisation
  • Supplier liaison & purchase-order processing
  • Insurance pre-authorisation submissions
  • Database maintenance & EMR data-entry – accuracy audits, record merging
