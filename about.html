About Our Company

Colak Health Management Ltd

Colak Health Management Ltd is a specialized healthcare staffing and management company providing comprehensive clinical support services to hospitals and medical clinics across the UK. Our team of qualified healthcare professionals delivers exceptional patient care, procedural assistance, and administrative support. We pride ourselves on our commitment to compliance, quality, and excellence in all aspects of healthcare management.

OUR EXPERTISE

  • Clinical & Procedural Support

    Pre-operative patient preparation, in-procedure assistance, post-operative wound care, sterilization & equipment turnover, daily theatre safety checks

  • Patient Coordination & Liaison

    Initial consultation scheduling, GDPR-compliant consent management, day-of-surgery patient reception, post-op follow-up

  • Media, Marketing & Content

    Clinical photography & videography, social-media asset creation, weekly content calendar planning, performance analytics

OUR STANDARDS

  • Compliance & Governance

    CQC audit preparation, data-protection impact assessments, incident reporting, disposable-tracking & stock-expiry checks, policy drafting/revision

  • Administrative Excellence

    Daily clinic list & rota production, monthly activity & revenue reporting, supplier liaison, insurance pre-authorization, database maintenance

  • Patient-Centered Care

    Personalized treatment plans, comprehensive aftercare, ongoing support throughout the hair restoration journey
